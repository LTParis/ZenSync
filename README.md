# ZenSync

Turn a [zenvents360](https://zenvents360.com) event into set folders in your own DJ library.

**[⬇ Download the latest release](https://github.com/LTParis/ZenSync/releases/latest)** — free, macOS 11+ or Windows.

---

This repository hosts the **downloads only**. The source is not public.

## What it does

Your clients plan their event in zenvents360 — the timeline, the must-plays, the
do-not-plays, their Music DNA. ZenSync reads that event and builds the crates you
actually play from:

- **Matches against your own files**, not a streaming service, using the paths your
  DJ software already knows.
- **Picks the right copy.** Your rated edit over the album cut, the clean version
  when the event calls for it, the original rather than a later re-record.
- **Files by the part of the evening.** VirtualDJ folders, Serato crates or a
  rekordbox library — with the do-not-play list as its own folder.

It also matches pasted lists and Spotify playlists against your library, which needs
no account at all.

## Requirements

- macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel) or
- Windows 10 or later
- VirtualDJ or Serato
- A zenvents360 account for the event connector — [start a free trial](https://app.zenvents360.com/register)

Signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.
